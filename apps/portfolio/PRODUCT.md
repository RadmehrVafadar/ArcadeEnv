# Portfolio product

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

The portfolio introduces Radmehr Vafadar through a landing page, blog, resume, and interactive pixel-art museum inside an optional 3D arcade cabinet.
The museum lets visitors explore, inspect an exhibit, and open its destination without progression gates.

## Users

Visitors exploring the owner's work on desktop, phone, or tablet, either directly or inside the cabinet iframe.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The museum retains the existing player sprite, keyboard controls, pointer D-pad, and base-aware exit navigation.
Its initial exhibit is the LinkedIn mirror; the other former projects are removed without placeholders.
Tiled owns world layout and authored collision, while TypeScript content records own exhibit descriptions and links.
The surrounding portfolio, cabinet, blog, and resume remain independent of the game runtime.

## Brand Commitments

The museum uses new artwork inspired by the original warm wood, burgundy carpet, golden lamps, and whimsical artifacts.
The original character and local Press Start 2P font are retained.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and touch actions are equivalent.
Controls remain outside the game canvas, with visible focus states, readable HTML dialogue, and a persistent Menu link.
