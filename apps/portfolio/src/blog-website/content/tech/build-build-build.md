# Build. Build. Build.

“Yesterday is history, tomorrow is a mystery, but today is a gift.
That is why it is called the present.” - Master Oogway

I’ve loved this quote since I first heard it as a kid, watching cartoons and eating cereal before school.

Lately, I’ve been thinking about it for a different reason: even in uncertain times, there is opportunity.

It’s easy to follow the news and spiral.
Are our jobs safe?
Is AI secure enough?
Will it replace software engineers?

These are real concerns.
But when we focus only on what we might lose, we can miss what we can do right now.

AI gives software engineers more ways to build.
Ideas that once felt out of reach are easier to explore.
You can try something, learn from it, and keep going.

- That idea you had as a kid? Build it.
- The video game you always wanted to make? Start making it.
- The business you’ve been thinking about? Take the first step.

I’m not saying everything is fine or that you should ignore your problems.
The uncertainty is real, especially for junior developers and new graduates.
You can take those challenges seriously and still make something you care about.

We don’t know how long today’s tools, prices, or access will last.
But we have them now.
That is an opportunity worth using.

While the world debates what AI will mean for the future, give yourself room to help shape it.

**Build something you’ve always wanted to exist.**
