# Blog product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors browse Rad's published writing by topic and read individual articles.
Rad authors articles as Markdown files, with the intent to adapt existing notes.

## Product Purpose

Provide a stripped-down, comfortable reading experience organized like folders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The home title is `rad's blog`, followed by category links.
The portfolio Blog link opens the reader in a new browser tab outside the arcade.
Folders use `../` navigation, a right-aligned title within a centered column, and article listings.
The initial categories are tech, hobbies, and work, with no published sample articles.
Standard Markdown is supported; Obsidian conversion is outside this change.
The blog retains the portfolio entry URL and existing static deployment.

## Brand Commitments

The approved treatment is warm paper, charcoal text, serif articles, and small monospace folder links.
Review uses the working page and desktop/mobile screenshots.
This context applies only to the blog, not the portfolio landing page or arcade.
